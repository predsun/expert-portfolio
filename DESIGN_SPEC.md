# Expert Portfolio - Design Specification & Content Strategy

## Design Philosophy
This website embodies the aesthetic of a **senior investment management professional's personal brand**, drawing inspiration from McKinsey, BlackRock, and Goldman Sachs. The design prioritizes **trust, professionalism, order, and long-term vision** through restraint, precision, and high-quality typography.

### Core Design Principles
1. **Minimalism with Authority:** Extensive whitespace, clean lines, and deliberate composition convey confidence and expertise.
2. **Rational Hierarchy:** Typography, spacing, and color are used strategically to guide attention and establish information priority.
3. **International Sophistication:** Bilingual support, neutral color palette, and refined typography appeal to global audiences.
4. **Subtle Motion:** Micro-interactions (fade-in, gentle transitions) enhance usability without distraction.

## Interaction & Animation Guidelines

### Micro-Interactions
1. **Hover Effects:** Buttons and links show subtle color change or underline
2. **Card Hover:** Slight shadow increase, subtle lift (2-4px)
3. **Focus States:** Clear focus ring for keyboard navigation
4. **Active States:** Navigation items highlight with underline or background color

### Page Transitions
- **Fade-In:** Content fades in on page load (300ms)
- **Scroll Animations:** Sections fade in as they enter viewport (subtle, not distracting)
- **Smooth Scroll:** Anchor links scroll smoothly to target sections

### Loading States
- **Skeleton Screens:** (If applicable) Subtle gray placeholders
- **Spinners:** Minimal, navy-colored spinner

## Bilingual Support
- **Language Toggle:** Top-right corner of navigation
- **Default Language:** Chinese (CN)
- **Supported Languages:** Chinese (CN) and English (EN)
- **Implementation:** React Context + i18n library or custom state management
- **Content:** All pages have complete CN and EN versions

## SEO & Performance
- **Meta Tags:** Title, description, keywords for each page
- **Structured Data:** Schema.org markup for professional profile
- **Image Optimization:** Lazy loading, responsive images
- **Font Loading:** Google Fonts with font-display: swap
- **Performance Target:** Lighthouse score 90+

---

## Accessibility
- **Color Contrast:** WCAG AA compliance (4.5:1 for body text)
- **Keyboard Navigation:** All interactive elements accessible via keyboard
- **ARIA Labels:** Proper labels for buttons, links, and form elements
- **Focus Indicators:** Clear, visible focus rings
- **Alt Text:** Descriptive alt text for all images
